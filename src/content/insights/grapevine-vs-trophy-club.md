Grapevine and Trophy Club are both close to Grapevine Lake and the SH 114 corridor, but they're different places to live. Grapevine is a historic Main Street town with lake recreation and quick access to DFW Airport. Trophy Club is a quieter golf-community town between Roanoke and Flower Mound, where life follows the rhythm of a planned community.

If you're relocating, or you just know you want North DFW without knowing which town, this comparison helps you and Diego narrow the list before you spend a weekend touring.

## Grapevine at a glance

Grapevine combines **historic Main Street character, Grapevine Lake recreation, and DFW Airport access**. Downtown is a real walkable district with restaurants, tasting rooms and events, and TEXRail connects it to the airport and Fort Worth. Outside downtown, you'll compare established family subdivisions such as the streets around Dove Crossing, pockets near the lake, and more typical suburban homes toward Grapevine Mills and the freeways.

The housing varies a lot: downtown character homes, lake-adjacent streets and 1980s–1990s subdivisions are different products. Grapevine can also feel busier and more visitor-oriented on weekends.

## Trophy Club at a glance

Trophy Club is a polished town known for **golf-community and master-planned character**: landscaped streets, amenity-focused living, and a quieter pace than Grapevine's tourism core. It sits near Roanoke and Flower Mound, with reach toward Alliance, I-35W and SH 114.

The town sits where Denton and Tarrant counties overlap, so two addresses in Trophy Club can fall under different taxing entities and have very different daily drives. Most of the market is resale in planned pockets, where the level of updates, the lot's orientation and HOA rules matter as much as square footage.

## What to weigh before you tour

1. **Drive your real peak-hour route.** Grapevine favors the 121 / 114 / airport direction, and Trophy Club favors 114 and I-35W toward Alliance. Test the trip from the actual homes you like.
2. **Think about how you'll actually spend your weekends.** If you'd use Main Street and the lake every week, Grapevine pays off. If you'd rarely use them, Trophy Club's quieter pace may suit you better.
3. **Read the HOA covenants for the specific property.** Dues, architectural review and rental rules aren't the same across either town.
4. **Pull taxing entities by address.** Don't assume a neighbor's bill, or a citywide average, applies to your street, especially with Trophy Club's county overlap.
5. **Check noise, flight paths and flood exposure where they apply.** Being close to the airport and the lake is a plus, but walk the yard and ask about the specific pocket.

## FAQs

**Grapevine or Trophy Club — which is better?**
Neither one is better for everyone. Grapevine fits buyers who want DFW Airport access, the lake and a lively Main Street. Trophy Club fits buyers who want a quieter golf-community feel near Roanoke and Flower Mound. Let your commute and how you spend your weekends decide.

**Which is closer to DFW Airport?**
Grapevine sits right next to the airport, and TEXRail runs from downtown. Trophy Club is farther north, so the trip takes longer. Test the drive from the specific homes you like at the times you'd actually travel.

**How do taxes, MUDs and HOAs compare?**
They vary by address in both towns, and Trophy Club straddles Denton and Tarrant counties. Confirm the taxing entities, any MUD or special district, and HOA dues for each property. This page doesn't list figures.
