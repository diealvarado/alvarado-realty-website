Lewisville and Highland Village are next-door neighbors on the Grapevine Lake and I-35E corridor in Denton County. Both offer lake recreation close at hand and a workable commute toward Dallas, the Mid-Cities, and DFW Airport — but they feel different on an ordinary Tuesday.

If you are relocating, moving up, or buying a long-term hold, this side-by-side helps you decide which city to tour first and what to check before you write an offer.

## Lewisville at a glance

Lewisville is a growing Denton County city known for a **diverse housing mix** and practical freeway access. Its market is shaped by resale variety more than by a single master-planned brand: Old Town character with a civic, walkable center; lake-corridor pockets where weekend recreation is part of the value; freeway-adjacent residential streets that prioritize drive time; and established suburban streets where update level and lot size matter as much as square footage.

Buyers usually choose Lewisville for **product breadth and price-band flexibility** — more types of homes across a wider range of price points than a compact village usually offers — while keeping lake access and a short hop to I-35E.

## Highland Village at a glance

Highland Village is a compact Denton County suburb on the Grapevine Lake corridor with a **village-center feel**. Errands cluster around a local retail core, lakeside recreation is close, and the residential cadence is quieter than a busy freeway corridor. Housing leans toward established suburban and lakeside-leaning streets rather than a wide spread of product types.

Buyers usually choose Highland Village for a **tighter geography and a calmer daily rhythm**, accepting a narrower range of inventory in exchange.

## What to weigh before you tour

1. **Price-point range and housing mix** — Lewisville generally spans a broader range of price points and home types; Highland Village is more concentrated. Decide what product you want before you compare listings.
2. **Lake access** — both cities touch Grapevine Lake, but how close and how usable the water is changes street by street. If the lake is the reason you are moving, prioritize pockets that make it easy.
3. **I-35E commute** — drive *your* peak-hour route from the specific homes you like. Freeway-adjacent streets save time but can add road noise.
4. **Taxes and MUDs vary by address** — taxing entities, any MUD or special district, and HOA dues differ by property, even within one city. Pull the current figures for each home.
5. **Schools by address** — campus assignments are address-specific and can change. Verify the zone for the exact home before you finalize.

## FAQs

**Lewisville or Highland Village — which is better?**  
Neither is better for everyone. Lewisville offers more variety in housing and freeway convenience; Highland Village offers a compact, village-centered residential feel near the lake. Match the city to your commute, budget range, and weekends.

**Is Lewisville a good place to buy a rental property?**  
It can be, depending on the specific street and property. Underwrite rent context, repair costs, HOA rental rules, and taxes for the exact address — this site does not promise yields or rents.

**Do taxes and MUDs work the same in both cities?**  
No. Taxing entities, any MUD or special district, and HOA dues vary by address. Confirm them for each property you are serious about.

**How should we think about schools?**  
Campus assignments are address-specific and can change. Verify the zone for any home before you finalize — this site does not invent school placements.
