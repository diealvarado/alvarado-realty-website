## What the latest DFW numbers show

Official October 2026 metro figures were not published as of November 11. The latest complete month is September 2026, from the NTREIS monthly MLS summary prepared by the Texas Real Estate Research Center (dated October 7, 2026). These are Dallas–Fort Worth–Arlington single-family MLS figures. They are not a value opinion on your house, and city-level conditions can differ.

On the sale side, single-family homes had **4.3 months of inventory**. The median close price was **$385,000**, down 1% from September 2025. Closed sales fell 7% to 6,759. Homes sold at **93.9% of list price** after **62 days on market**.

On the lease side, the market was tighter. Single-family leases had **2.2 months of inventory**, with 7,503 active listings, down 11% from a year earlier. The median lease was **$2,300**, unchanged from September 2025. Closed leases fell 5% to 3,067. Homes leased at **96.4% of list price** after **42 days on market**.

In September, then, it was generally easier to fill a typical single-family rental than to sell a typical single-family home quickly and at full ask. That is a snapshot, not a reason to rent. Buyers still have room to negotiate, and sellers who price correctly still sell. The 30-year fixed mortgage averaged **7.18%** as of November 5, 2026, down for a third straight week, but still above the 6.22% average a year earlier (Freddie Mac). Run the decision at today's rate, not the rate you hope for.

## Questions to ask before you decide

**1. What is this home supposed to do for you over the next few years?**
If you might move back, want to keep the house in the family, or are building a long-term hold, leasing can keep the asset. If you need the equity, want a clean break, or do not want landlord work, selling is often the cleaner path. You and Diego will start with that goal, then test it against the numbers.

**2. What would this house likely sell for, and what would it likely lease for?**
Metro medians are a backdrop, not your price. You and Diego will pull recent sales and leased comps near the home, and factor in condition, updates, lot, and the pocket — a [Flower Mound](/areas/flower-mound/) street does not price like a [Lewisville](/areas/lewisville/) street, and [Coppell](/areas/coppell/), [Grapevine](/areas/grapevine/), [Euless](/areas/euless/), and [Bedford](/areas/bedford/) each behave differently. For a sale-side starting point, see the [home value](/home-value/) form.

**3. What will it cost to keep?**
A rental still has a mortgage (if you have one), property taxes, insurance, HOA dues, maintenance, and vacant days between tenants. This page does not invent those figures. You and Diego can help you list the property-side costs; your CPA should review the tax treatment.

**4. Are you ready to be a landlord after the lease is signed?**
If you lease with Diego, you and Diego will price the listing, market it, screen every applicant against the same written criteria, and prepare the lease. Once the lease is signed, day-to-day care of the home and the tenant relationship stays with you. Diego does **not** offer ongoing property management. If you do not want that role, selling may fit better. See [leasing and tenant placement](/leasing/).

**5. What do the documents allow?**
HOA or deed restrictions sometimes limit rentals, minimum lease terms, or how many homes may be leased. A loan may have occupancy terms worth reading with your lender or attorney. Confirm those constraints before you prepare the house for tenants.

**6. Which questions belong with your CPA or attorney?**
Tax treatment can change when a principal residence becomes a rental, and a sale can raise capital-gains questions. Texas school districts must provide a **$140,000** residence homestead exemption, and it applies only to a qualifying principal residence, not to a rental (Texas Comptroller; Tax Code § 11.13). A temporary **20%** cap on annual appraisal increases for eligible non-homestead real property expires **December 31, 2026**. Unless the Legislature extends it, that cap will not apply in tax year 2027, and eligible properties can be appraised at full market value, subject to the statute's rules. Homesteaded homes are outside that cap and have a separate limitation. Confirm eligibility with your CPA. Diego is not a tax advisor. For a property-side conversation and a specialist referral, see [real estate wealth planning](/wealth-planning/).

**Is now a better time to rent or to sell in DFW?**
Neither one is automatically better. In September 2026, single-family leases were tighter than single-family sales, and the median lease held steady while the median sale price was slightly lower than a year earlier. Your house and whether you want to be a landlord matter more than the metro snapshot.
