## Argyle, TX

Argyle is a town that many buyers associate with **larger lots, open space, and a more rural edge** than the denser suburbs to the south and east. Homes vary widely: some streets offer acreage-leaning properties with room for privacy, outbuildings, or simply space between neighbors, while other areas feel more like conventional newer suburban neighborhoods.

Because lot size, deed restrictions, utilities, and HOA presence can change from one property to the next, Argyle shopping is very much a *property-by-property* exercise. Confirm what is allowed on the land, how utilities are provided, and whether an HOA applies before you fall for the view.

## Lantana, TX

Lantana is best known as a **master-planned community** experience: a consistent streetscape, community amenities, HOA standards, and newer homes laid out for families who want convenience and a packaged neighborhood feel. Buyers who choose Lantana usually value amenities and predictability over acreage.

Master-planned living comes with its own homework: HOA dues, architectural rules, and any special-district or MUD obligations are part of the real monthly cost. Review them for the specific address before you write an offer.
